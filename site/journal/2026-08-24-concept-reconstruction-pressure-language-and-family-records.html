Concept Reconstruction Experiments stopped being a proposal this interval. They ran, they exposed missing language semantics, the language answered with Source Profile 0.4 and a plural backend family, and Forge, Fabric, Commons, Harness, and Control grew the records needed to make those experiments durable.

Language: CRE pressure exposed what Source Profile 0.3 could not say

The Concept Reconstruction loop was adopted as the language's own pressure mechanism, not an external audit: reconstructing a bounded concept from its evidence was treated as a repeatable way to find where MNCS Language lacked semantics (documentation:epi13/mncs-language:49).

The pressure worked. CRE-3, which needs bounded retry, stayed blocked because Source Profile 0.3 could express finite outcomes but not iteration without source unrolling or recursion; Profile 0.4 added verification-visible bounded iteration to unblock it while older profiles kept their syntax and fingerprints (rfc:epi13/mncs-language:54, rfc:epi13/mncs-language:53).

Local working-tree evidence shows how recent this is: the Profile 0.4 bounded-iteration notes and the compiler architecture assessment exist only as local documents so far, describing Profile 0.4 as a bounded slice rather than a completed compiler (control:local_notes:72e612978f8d02100aaf1971, control:local_notes:40749c15c774545950d43220).

Backends: plurality became an invariant, then gained realizations

Before more experiments could run, the compiler's shape had to change. RFC 0041 established backend plurality as a first-class invariant precisely so the portable WASM path would not harden into accidental architectural centrality; at that point it was still the only privileged executable route (rfc:epi13/mncs-language:50, control:local_notes:fce1b3fd489bac021f47e573).

The language project then owned its backend experiments directly and widened the family from two realizations to many: LLVM, C11, and Cranelift joined portable WASM and research bytecode as adapters in a backend-neutral registry, with translation validation kept outside any single target (documentation:epi13/mncs-language:55, commit:epi13/mncs-language:29bf01661fab, architecture:epi13/mncs-language:51).

Compiler studies now emit stable pipeline, target, and artifact identities as producer-native Family Record references, so each realization can be cited without collapsing backends into one story (documentation:epi13/mncs-language:52). WASM remains one backend among several; nothing in this interval made it the definition of MNCS realization.

The family grew records so experiments survive their processes

Experiments only matter if their records outlive them. Commons implemented the Family Record Spine: canonical ConceptExperiment records with exact producer references, a replication record builder, and deliberately neutral attempts relations so UNKNOWN replication outcomes are representable instead of being forced into PASS or FAIL (documentation:epi13/MNCS-Commons:19, documentation:epi13/MNCS-Commons:20, documentation:epi13/MNCS-Commons:21).

Each subsystem kept its own meaning while gaining bounded surfaces: Forge binds evaluations to identity-bearing Concept Experiments without claiming correctness authority (documentation:epi13/mncs-forge-mcp:39); Fabric projects durable execution attempts for family records and defines the transport boundary for concept experiments (documentation:epi13/mncs-fabric:63, documentation:epi13/mncs-fabric:61); Control coordinates durable experiment lifecycle and replication without owning scientific truth (documentation:epi13/mncs-control-mcp:35, documentation:epi13/mncs-control-mcp:30); Harness exposes exact actor provenance for who and what executed each role (documentation:epi13/mncs-harness:51). The standards side defined how this evidence can later flow into MNCS assurance cases without turning MNCS itself into the development process (documentation:epi13/machine-native-complexity-standard:67).

Durable experiments ran, including the ones that failed

Control's durable experiment coordinator recorded five completed experiments inside this interval. Their shapes differ in ways worth keeping visible: one finished four turns with four successes; another logged forty-eight turns with zero successful outcomes; two others carried partial failures through recovery paths (control:experiments:05539b00638cc027b039771b, control:experiments:12213d525c1d00db7e37d939, control:experiments:d1d3fa8a21f829ee86ca2100, control:experiments:f39212bdbb2d0a401029b6a3).

These records carry claim boundaries stating that Control coordinates the lifecycle and does not judge scientific meaning, which is why this entry reports them rather than concluding from them. A forty-eight-turn run ending with no successful turns is part of the developmental record here, exactly like a merged result would be.

Orientation work continued around the experiments

Atlas itself shipped the Journal Maintainer contract, implementation, and editorial hardening during this interval, making machine-maintained entries possible under explicit non-normative authority (documentation:epi13/mncs-atlas:10, documentation:epi13/mncs-atlas:11). The standards repository bootstrapped a family deployment system and refreshed the ecosystem entry point, reflecting that MNCS is now a composed family rather than a single package (rfc:epi13/machine-native-complexity-standard:66).

Unresolved questions

Whether the backend family's new realizations hold up as CRE pressure increases, or whether plurality is currently breadth without equally deep lowering contracts per backend (rfc:epi13/mncs-language:50, documentation:epi13/mncs-language:55).

How quickly local-only assessment documents — compiler architecture, backend plurality, Profile 0.4 evidence — should be promoted into committed repository records so they stop depending on operator context for visibility (control:local_notes:40749c15c774545950d43220, control:local_notes:fce1b3fd489bac021f47e573).

Whether durable-experiment records that end COMPLETED with zero successful turns should feed Forge replication attempts automatically or wait for explicit operator interpretation (control:experiments:12213d525c1d00db7e37d939).
